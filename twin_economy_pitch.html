Abstract

A Human Owned Behavioural Data Economy

Twinlytics is a research-led platform where individuals build AI-powered digital twins from their own behavioural data and directly participate in the economic value that data generates. This document details the problem, proposed approach, evidence base, and strategic 5-year roadmap.

Approach

A two-sided marketplace built on consented AI twins

Individuals build a personal digital twin from reflective diary entries and behavioural inputs. Organisations query populations of consented twins for rapid, GDPR-compliant market research and policy simulation.

Launch Strategy

Four-phase strategy to reach critical mass

Phase 1 · Months 1 to 4

Single-User Utility First

The app offers rich personal value (journaling, emotional tracking, AI reflection insights) before marketplace activation, seeding 200 to 500 active users via university networks.

Milestone: 500 active diary users via Fraunhofer research ecosystem.
Phase 2 · Months 3 to 8

Synthetic Twin Seeding

Validated synthetic twins from open research datasets (capped at 30% total pool) allow early B2B clients to run queries while real user supply builds up.

Milestone: Smooth transition from synthetic to 100% verified real twins.
Phase 3 · Months 6 to 12

Concierge Pilot Matching

Hand-match early enterprise clients with target demographic twin cohorts, providing tailored query assistance and formal validation reports.

Milestone: 5 to 10 paid enterprise pilot case studies.
Phase 4 · Month 12 Onward

Autonomous Flywheel Activation

With 1,000+ active twins and recurring corporate subscriptions, growth transitions from direct sales to organic word-of-mouth and self-serve API access.

Milestone: Self-sustaining economic data network.

Strategic Analysis

Moats and risk mitigation strategies

Sources of Structural Defensibility

  • Data Network Effect: Larger user base produces higher twin diversity, driving higher enterprise query demand.
  • Longitudinal Depth: Months of personal diary context build high-fidelity twins impossible to clone overnight.
  • Proprietary IP: Patent-pending LLM distortion removal & bias mitigation techniques.
  • Consent Infrastructure: Complex cryptographic & regulatory compliance framework that creates high switching costs.
  • Institutional Credibility: Peer-reviewed publications and Fraunhofer research backing.

Risk Mitigation Architecture

User Psychological Safety

Granular opt-in domain control; zero predictive modeling without explicit double-opt-in consent.

Data Integrity & Anti-Gaming

Verification pipelines, logic consistency checks, and quality scoring certificates detect synthetic spam.

Regulatory Future-Proofing

Native compliance with EU AI Act & GDPR, zero-knowledge data isolation, and full auditability.
