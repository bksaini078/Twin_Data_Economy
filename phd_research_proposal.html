PhD Scope

Human Digital Twins for Enterprise Decision Support

Shifting from static personas toward dynamic, interactive human digital twins grounded in authentic personal behavioral data for market research.

Pillar 01 · Contextual Grounding

Contextual Human Digital Twins

Grounding AI agents in real user reflective notes and survey history to produce authentic, nuanced decision responses instead of generic LLM outputs.

Pillar 02 · Stakeholder Utility

Enterprise Platform Validation

Investigating explicit stakeholder requirements, workflow integration, and empirically validating that synthetic twin platforms solve a problem worth solving.

Pillar 03 · Data Economy

Consented Data Marketplace

Creating a privacy-preserving behavioral data economy where users own their personal digital twins and earn automated payouts for participating in research.

Research Questions

Three Focused Research Questions (RQ1 - RQ3)

Structuring the remaining PhD roadmap around technical distortions, stakeholder requirements, and digital twin market economics.

RQ1 · Technical Distortions

What systematic response distortions and biases emerge in LLM persona simulation, and how can they be mitigated?

Quantifying prompt sensitivity, cognitive bias propagation, and hallucination bounds in simulated survey responses to establish reliability guidelines.

RQ2 · Requirements & Evaluation

What are enterprise stakeholder requirements, and how does Twinlytics perform compared to traditional panels?

Empirically evaluating decision speed, confidence, insight quality, and workflow integration through 15-20 stakeholder interviews and a comparative pilot.

RQ3 · Twin Economics & Use Cases

What new business values, market opportunities, and economic viability model emerge from human-controlled AI twins?

Formalizing digital twin use cases, market creation opportunities, value capture architectures, and twin economy feasibility.
